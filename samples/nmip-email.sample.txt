Some key notes on this new grant programme which opened this week, as covered in today's briefing. For any client considering applying they should watch the recorded briefing (once available), as there are a lot of specific details and rules/requirements to take note of.

£12m total funding available
18-30 month projects
Grant size £500k-£1m
Closing date 28.10.2026 11am
Project start 1.2.2027 and must close by 31.7.2029
10 scored questions, each with 400 word count limit
Must collaborate and must be business-led
Must include an SME
Must explain the opportunity for the materials innovation to be applied and also describe any foreseen barriers to adoption
Must show the route to scale and how it will support UK advanced manufacturing
Must include at least 1 named end user (who is operating within one of the 8 IS sectors) -- note, this end user can be a funded or non-funded collaborator, could be and end user on the project advisory board. RTOs and academics / academic organisations are not permitted. Government departments or regulators could only be considered as an end user with adequate justification.
Strict requirements to demonstrate having an end user - must have a clear, defined role within the project governance or work plan (so, for instance, having only 'letters of support' etc from an end user are not sufficient)

Focus areas: must fit one of these 4 themes and at least one of the associated sub-themes within each:

- Future healthcare -- Biocompatible and Regenerative Materials / Bioelectronic Materials
- Structural material innovation - Sustainability / Surface Engineering / Design and Deployment / Extreme Environments
- Power electronics and Connectivity - Wide and Ultra Wide Bandgap Materials / Advanced Connectivity Materials
- Metamaterials and Metasurfaces - Future Telecommunications and Advanced Connectivity / Convergent Electronic-Photonic Technologies / Thermal, Acoustic and Mechanical Metamaterials / Energy Security and Net Zero / Healthcare
